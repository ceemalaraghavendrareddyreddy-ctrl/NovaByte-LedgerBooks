Step 2 — Connect it to MRA (aim: 15–20 seconds)
⚠ This step is easy to forget in rehearsal — a brand-new company has NO MRA connection configured until you do this. Skip it and the invoice you&apos;re about to create will show no MRA status at all, with no error to explain why.
Click Settings in the left sidebar (under Admin).
Scroll to &quot;MRA API URL&quot; and &quot;MRA API Key&quot;.
Paste in http://localhost:5059 and the key from your demo_mra_key.txt file.
Click &quot;Save Settings&quot;.
SAY: &quot;This one-time link is what turns on automatic compliance — from here every invoice fiscalizes itself.&quot;
Step 3 — Create the customer (aim: 15 seconds)
Sales → Customers → &quot;+ New Customer&quot;.
Name: Baobab Textiles Ltd — leave every other field blank, click Save Customer.
Step 4 — Create and post the invoice (aim: 40–50 seconds)
Sales → Invoices → New Invoice.
Customer: Baobab Textiles Ltd.
Leave Invoice Date and Due Date at their defaults.
Memo: Consulting services - August.
On the line: leave the Item dropdown at &quot;-- manual line --&quot; (a brand-new company has no items yet — that&apos;s expected, not a bug). Description: Business advisory - August. Qty: 1. Unit Price: 5000. Leave Taxable checked.
Watch the Subtotal / VAT / Total boxes update live as you type — the screen will read Subtotal 5000.00, VAT 750.00, Total 5750.00 (LedgerBooks doesn&apos;t use thousand-separator commas on screen).
Click &quot;Save &amp; Post Invoice&quot;.
SAY: &quot;That single click just posted a balanced double-entry journal entry AND sent it to MRA for fiscalisation — both in one step.&quot;
Step 5 — Show it landed on the MRA side (aim: 15 seconds)
On the invoice detail page you&apos;re now viewing, point at the line under the header: &quot;MRA: OFFLINE · [invoice number] · IRN [...]&quot; — this appeared automatically, nothing was clicked to make it happen.
Switch to your already-open MRA_TaxInvoice_System tab (logged in as demo_reference), click Invoices in the nav, and point at the newest row — same customer, same total, already there.
SAY: &quot;I didn&apos;t touch that system — LedgerBooks called it directly the moment I saved the invoice.&quot;
Step 6 — Switch companies to show isolation (aim: 15 seconds)
Back in LedgerBooks, log out and log back in as admin / admin123 (this account has access to more than one company, so the switcher will appear in the sidebar).
Click the company switcher at the top of the sidebar, and pick a different company from the list.
Land on that company&apos;s Dashboard — different invoices, different balances, completely separate.
SAY: &quot;This is what it looks like when you have fifteen clients — one login, a dropdown, zero chance of one client ever seeing another&apos;s numbers.&quot;

01 — Where it actually stood

The gap was smaller than the first pass suggested

The original roadmap was written from two demo documents describing one transaction flow — not the real codebase. Once the actual code was read, most of "Phase 1" and half of "Phase 2" turned out to already exist.

🟢 Already there — moat, not a gap

  • Per-company invoice numbering and fully isolated multi-tenant books
  • Live fiscalization bridge to MRA_TaxInvoice_System (IRN issuance, offline-mode fallback)
  • Vendor bills, purchase orders, vendor credits, customer credit memos
  • Bank reconciliation, statement import, deposits, transfers
  • Budgets, P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, VAT return, custom report builder
  • Aged receivables and payables, inventory/COGS, audit log

🟠 Genuinely missing — the real gap

  • Recurring / subscription invoices — shipped
  • Fixed asset register & depreciation — shipped
  • Multi-currency (core AR/AP) — shipped
  • Payroll — no module existed at all — bridged to an external system rather than rebuilt
What didn't change: nothing below touched the fiscalization bridge or the per-company isolation model — every addition is new tables/accounts/routes, not a rewrite of what already worked.

05 — How it's actually built

The real implementation, not the earlier speculation

Recurring invoices

RecurringInvoice + RecurringInvoiceLine tables; a daily APScheduler job per company generates due invoices through the same post_invoice() path as a manual one.

Fixed assets

Asset + AssetDepreciationEntry; new accounts 1590 Accumulated Depreciation, 4910 Gain/Loss on Disposal, 6400 Depreciation Expense.

Multi-currency

currency + exchange_rate on Invoice/Bill/Payment/VendorPayment; a new 4920 Realized Gain/Loss on Exchange account absorbs the rate difference between when a receivable was booked and when it was actually settled.

Payroll bridge

POST /api/v1/payroll/import, authenticated by a per-company key the payroll system presents — the reverse direction of the existing MRA bridge. New liability accounts 2300–2370 for PAYE/CSG/NSF/HRDC/PRGF/levy/net-salaries/deductions payable.

Also fixed along the way, in the external payroll engine itself: a December 13th-month bonus was being annualized alongside recurring pay, overtaxing every employee's biggest paycheck of the year. Replaced with a proper marginal-rate calculation, hand-verified against the slab math. Added a net-pay floor so statutory deductions are never reduced but discretionary ones (loan installments) yield first — net pay can no longer go negative.
06 — Deliberately left open

Small, flagged — not forgotten

  • Estimates, Purchase Orders, Credit Memos, Vendor Credits don't carry currency yet — they stay base-currency-only. Low urgency since none of them post to the ledger directly except through the Invoice/Bill path, which is covered.
  • No admin visibility into the two scheduled jobs (recurring invoices, asset depreciation) — they run silently at 06:00/06:05 daily. Worth a status page once there are more than two jobs to watch.
  • Payroll pushes are manual — someone clicks "Push to LedgerBooks" per Finalized run. Could auto-push on Finalize instead; left manual deliberately so a correction can be made before it hits the GL.

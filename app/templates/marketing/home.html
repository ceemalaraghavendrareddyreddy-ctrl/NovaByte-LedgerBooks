Modules

01

Multi-Company Ledger

Separate books for every registered entity, each with its own chart of accounts, users, and settings — one login.

02

Double-Entry Core

Every transaction posts as a balanced journal entry. Debits and credits must match to the cent before it saves.

03

Multi-Currency & FX

MUR, USD, EUR, GBP, ZAR, or INR on any invoice or bill, with realized gain/loss posted automatically on settlement.

04

Sales & Purchases

Invoices, credit memos, vendor bills, and payments — plus recurring invoices for retainer-style customers.

05

Fixed Assets

Straight-line depreciation scheduled and posted automatically each month, through to disposal.

06

Payroll Bridge

A signed API endpoint your payroll system pushes finalized runs to — one balanced entry per run, no re-entry.

07

MRA E-Invoicing

Invoices and credit memos fiscalize automatically, with a retry queue for anything that fails the first time.

08

Share via WhatsApp

Every invoice, bill, and credit memo gets a signed, time-limited link a customer can open without logging in.

09

Bank Reconciliation

Match statement lines to ledger entries and see exactly what's still outstanding before month-end.

10

Reports

Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, and budget-vs-actual — always current, never a month-end scramble.

11

Role-Based Access & Audit Trail

Owners, accountants, and staff see only what their role allows. Every change is logged, with who and when.

12

Backup & Restore

A full snapshot of the company's books, downloadable on demand and restorable to the exact row.

About

Software built around one rule: it has to balance

NovaByte LedgerBooks started from a simple frustration with spreadsheet bookkeeping in a multi-currency, multi-company setting: it's easy to make the numbers look right without them actually being right. Everything in the product exists to close that gap.

What guides how it's built

01

Accuracy first

No entry saves unless it balances. No shortcut skips that rule, including the automated ones — payroll, depreciation, and recurring invoices all post through the same check.

02

Built portable

The same code runs on MySQL locally and Postgres in production, so a company's books are never tied to one host or one server.

03

No lock-in

A full backup is one click away, in an open format, restorable to the exact row — your data leaves as easily as it arrived.

04

Compliance by design

MRA fiscalization and audit trails aren't bolted on afterward — they're part of how an invoice or a change gets recorded in the first place.

Only worth turning on once more than one person enters bills. When enabled, a new bill at or above the threshold is held for an owner to approve before it posts to the ledger — see Purchases → Bill approvals.

{# ── Invoice approval workflow ────────────────────────────── #}

Invoice Approvals

Mirrors Bill Approvals above, for outgoing invoices. When enabled, a new invoice at or above the threshold is held for an owner to approve before it posts to the ledger — see Sales → Invoice approvals.
